Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

At CSSMotionLab, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by CSSMotionLab and how we use it.

1. Log Files & Web Analytics

CSSMotionLab follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. All hosting companies do this as a part of hosting services' analytics. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, CSSMotionLab uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

3. Third-Party Advertising Partners & Adsterra

You may consult this list to find the Privacy Policy for each of the advertising partners of CSSMotionLab. Third-party ad servers or ad networks (such as Adsterra, Google AdSense, or ethical developer networks) use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on CSSMotionLab, which are sent directly to users' browser. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit.

Note that CSSMotionLab has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

4. Third Party Privacy Policies

CSSMotionLab's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions about how to opt-out of certain options.

We respect your privacy rights under CCPA and GDPR. Users have the right to request access, rectification, or erasure of any personal data collected. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us.
